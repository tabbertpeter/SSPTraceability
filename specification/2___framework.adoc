== Credible Simulation Process Framework

In the <<im-credibleSimulationProcessFramework>>, the _Credible Decision Process_, the _Credible Simulation Process_, and the _Credible Modeling Process_ are fundamentally separate and independent processes within the _Credible Simulation Process Framework_. This separation is crucial as these processes may be executed by different roles, organizations, or even companies. Despite their independence, these processes are interconnected through communication and information transfer.

1. Simulation Request:
   - When the need for a simulation arises within the _Credible Decision Process_, a _Simulation Request_ is issued.
   - This request initiates a _Credible Simulation Process_, which operates independently, applying the requirements and information provided with the request.

2. Modeling Request:
   - If the _Credible Simulation Process_ requires new simulation models, a _Modeling Request_ is issued.
   - This request triggers a _Credible Modeling Process_, which operates independently of the simulation process and applies the requirements and information provided with the request.

3. Modeling Delivery:
   - The result of the _Credible Modeling Process_, namely the simulation models, is delivered to the initiating _Credible Simulation Process_.
   - This transfer of information is called _Modeling Delivery_.

4. Simulation Delivery:
   - The results of the _Credible Simulation Process_, i.e., the simulation results, are delivered to the initiating _Credible Decision Process_.
   - This transfer is called _Simulation Delivery_.

For more information on the processes of the Credible Simulation Process Framework, see <<SETLEVEL>>.

[#sec-credibleDecisionProcess]
=== Credible Decision Process

The Credible Decision Process, as part of the Credible Simulation Process Framework, consists of five successive phases. It begins with the analysis of the decision to be made, continues through the preparation of the decision, and culminates in the decision itself. The preparation phase includes all activities necessary to obtain and evaluate the information on which the decision will be based. This information can include simulation results. The <<im-credibleDecisionProcess>> illustrates this process.

[#im-credibleDecisionProcess]
.Credible Decision Process
image::CredibleDecisionProcess.png[]

The phases of the Credible Decision Process are:
 
Analysis of Decision Task:: In this phase, the information required for the execution of the decision process is determined and analyzed.

Definition of Sub-Tasks:: In this phase, the sub-tasks (e.g., simulation or test tasks) required to obtain the information on which the decision should be based are defined and specified.
 
Performing Sub-Tasks:: In this phase, the sub-tasks (e.g., simulation or test tasks) are carried out. The phase starts with the simulation request and ends with the delivery of the simulation results from the perspective of the Credible Decision Process.
 
Evaluation:: In this phase, the decision-relevant information returned from the sub-tasks (e.g., simulation results) is evaluated from the point of view of the Credible Decision Process.
 
Fulfillment:: The purpose of this phase is to decide whether the results fulfill the original objectives of the decision task.

For more information on the Credible Decision Process, see <<SETLEVEL>>.

[#sec-credibleSimulationProcess]
=== Credible Simulation Process

The __Credible Simulation Process__, as part of the __Credible Simulation Process Framework__, consists of seven sequential phases. It begins with the analysis of the simulation task and progresses through the phases of simulation preparation, simulation execution, and evaluation of simulation results. The process continues until a decision is made as to whether the simulation results meet the simulation objectives, thereby completing the simulation task. The __Credible Simulation Process__ is triggered and initiated by a simulation request and returns its results, i.e., simulation results, to the requesting __Credible Decision Process__. The <<im-credibleSimulationProcess>> illustrates this process.

[#im-credibleSimulationProcess]
.Credible Simulation Process
image::CredibleSimulationProcess.png[]

The phases of the Credible Simulation Process are:

Analysis of Simulation Task:: In this phase, the information from the higher-level Credible Decision Process, provided as a Simulation Request, is analyzed and detailed with respect to the information needed in the subsequent phases of the Credible Simulation Process.

Definition of Simulation Requirements:: In this phase, the requirements for the individual components of the simulation setup and their interaction as well as the requirements for quality assurance are formulated in detail.

Design:: The purpose of this phase is to create all the specifications needed to implement the complete simulation setup.

Implementation:: The purpose of this phase is to implement the complete simulation setup with all its setup components as specified in the design specifications.

Simulation Execution:: The purpose of this phase is to run the simulation and record the simulation results.
It may also include any necessary post-processing. 

Evaluation:: The purpose of this phase is to evaluate the simulation results, i.e. to verify that the simulation results are plausible and robust.

Fulfillment:: The purpose of this phase is to confirm that the simulation results meet the original objectives of the simulation task.

For more information on the Credible Simulation Process, see <<SETLEVEL>>.

[#sec-credibleModelingProcess]
=== Credible Modeling Process

The__Credible Modeling Process__, as part of the __Credible Simulation Process Framework__, consists of five sequential phases. It begins with the analysis of the modeling task and proceeds through the stages of modeling preparation and the actual modeling phase. The modeling is referred to as implementation to be consistent with the corresponding naming in the __Credible Simulation Process__. The process concludes when a decision is made as to whether the simulation models meet the modeling objectives, thereby completing the modeling task. The __Credible Modeling Process__ is triggered and initiated by a __Modeling Request__ and returns its results, i.e., simulation models, to the requesting __Credible Simulation Process__.
